Which Key Did They Press?

A customer on the support line presses a key. The line never sees the key, only 8,000 samples a second of a short beep. Which key was it?

Source: Alan V. Oppenheim and Ronald W. Schafer, Discrete-Time Signal Processing, Prentice Hall, 1989, chapter 8. Next lesson: the FFT, where the key detector is too slow to run live on the chip. Lab drawing adapted from the Sample Hold Ladder study in the iwrzwr visual archive (MIT).
