Now a nasty surprise. The same tone, a quarter turn behind, scores zero. So take a backward wheel, turning clockwise, test with both its shadows, left-right and up-down, and treat the two sums as an arrow. Its length is four, whatever the phase. That length is the match.
Against the one thousand hertz wheel, the arrows close an octagon and end where they began: exactly zero. Any whole number of turns of difference does the same. So test only with whole-turn wheels. Each one is a bin: bin k makes k turns in a block of N samples. The list of their matches is the DFT.
A customer presses five. Two hundred and five samples, eight keypad bins. Bin twenty scores ninety percent of a full match, bin thirty-four ninety-two, the other six at thirteen percent or less. [surprise-oh] Row seven seventy, column thirteen thirty-six: the key five.
