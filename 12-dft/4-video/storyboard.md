# Storyboard: Which Key Did They Press?

**Problem:** the support line must tell which keypad key a customer pressed, from samples alone. A key is two tones at once, a low row tone and a high column tone (key 5: 770 Hz plus 1,336 Hz). **Audience:** software engineers with no signal processing background who did lessons 1 to 11. **Goal:** the viewer can say that a test tone multiplied with the recording and summed scores 4 for the right tone and exactly 0 for 1,000 Hz (eight samples of 2,000 Hz); that a tone a quarter turn behind hides from one test (sum 0), so the match is the length of the arrow made by both shadows' sums (4 whatever the phase); why it works (lesson 6's two half arrows laid tip to tail; the test wheel turns back, the forward half stands still and piles up to N ÷ 2, the mirror half turns 180° a sample against it, steps back and forth, and ends where it started); that a wheel making a different whole number of turns closes an octagon (exactly 0), so the bins are the whole-turn wheels, bin k making k turns in N samples at k × 8,000 ÷ N Hz, and the DFT is the list of their matches; that the frequency resolution 8,000 ÷ N decides whether close tones separate (N = 8: every row tone in bin 1; N = 40: 770 and 852 share bin 4; N = 205: each its own bin with an empty bin between); that key 5 at N = 205 scores 90% in bin 20 and 92% in bin 34, the other six keypad bins 13% or less; and that a 25% bar, cleared by exactly one row and one column, keeps silence from naming a key. **Emotion:** from "the line never sees a key" through the nasty surprise of a tone that hides, to the pleasure of two tall bars naming the key, then the sigh of a detector that is too slow.

Voice: the lesson 1 narrator, reused from its saved speaker (`01-signals-and-systems/4-video/vo/voice.pt`, designed as `female, young adult, high pitch, american accent`), for series continuity. agent-voice v0.2.0 (`agent-voice doctor`: ready, mps). Exact command, run through `voice.sh`, which takes the shared voice lock and releases it when the call ends:

```bash

```
