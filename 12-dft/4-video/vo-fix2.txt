The forward half stands still, like a fan under a strobe, and its arrows pile up: N over two, which is four in a block of eight. The mirror half turns twice as fast, steps back and forth, and ends where it started.
Against the one thousand hertz wheel, the arrows close an octagon and end where they began: exactly zero. Any other whole number of turns does the same. So test only with whole-turn wheels. Each one is a bin: bin k makes k turns in a block of N samples. The list of their matches is the DFT.
