Whistle at five thousand hertz into a phone, and the phone stores three thousand.
You have seen this before. On video, a fast wheel can seem to turn backwards.
A phone cannot store a wave. It measures the wave eight thousand times per second, and keeps only those dots.
Between two dots, it knows nothing. So it draws the slowest wave that fits them.
For a slow tone, the slowest fit is the true tone. For a fast tone, a slower wave fits the same dots.
The limit is half the sampling rate: four thousand hertz. Above it, a tone folds back, like a mirror. Five thousand is one thousand above, so it lands one thousand below.
After sampling, the true tone and the false one give the same numbers. You cannot undo it. So the phone network removes high tones before it samples.
Sampling sees only dots. Stay below half the sampling rate, or filter first.
