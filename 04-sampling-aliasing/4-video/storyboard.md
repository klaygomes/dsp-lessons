# Storyboard: The Whistle That Changed Its Mind

**Problem:** a hearing clinic plays a 5,000 Hz test whistle down the line, and every patient hears a clean, steady 3,000 Hz, so a patient who cannot hear 5,000 Hz still says "yes". **Audience:** software engineers with no signal processing background who did lessons 1 to 3. **Goal:** the viewer can say what the phone stores (a list of samples, 8,000 a second), why 5,000 Hz gives the same samples as 3,000 Hz (225° per sample lands on the mirror spot of 135°, so it looks like a slower wheel turning backwards), that the limit is half the sampling rate and a tone above it folds back to an alias, and why the fix is a filter before the sampler. **Emotion:** from "the line is lying fluently" to the surprise that two different waves share every dot, then the calm of an honest silence.

Voice: the lesson 1 narrator, reused from its saved speaker (`01-signals-and-systems/4-video/vo/voice.pt`, designed as `female, young adult, high pitch, american accent`), for series continuity. Exact command:

```bash
agent-voice script 04-sampling-aliasing/4-video/vo.txt --out 04-sampling-aliasing/4-video/vo --prompt 01-signals-and-systems/4-video/vo/voice.pt --speed 1.1 --temperature 0.8
```

`--speed 1.1 --temperature 0.8` is the user's choice by ear (1.3 was too fast). Two non-verbal tags: `[surprise-oh]` at the reveal ("So it lands one thousand below, on three thousand", line 7) and `[sigh]` before the next bug's question (line 10). No tag reaches the captions or the screen.

Visual code, the same marks as the page's figures (`2-figures/figures.mjs`, `lesson.css`): the true tone and the whistle as a dashed ink-soft wave; the 3,000 Hz tone as a solid coral wave (the same-dots figure's "solid coral: 3,000 Hz, what patients heard"); samples as ink stems with filled dots; the gaps between samples shaded in rule colour ("the phone looks away"); the wheel as an ink circle with the horizontal line through the centre dashed, the whistle's dot filled ink, the 3,000 Hz dot hollow, spokes dashed soft, the 225° turn as an ink arc arrow and the 135° clockwise view as a dotted coral arc arrow; cinema pictures as paper frames on a paper-2 film strip, the spoke in ink, the 15° clockwise step as a coral arrow; the tent and the fold strip in ink with the 5,000 → 3,000 alias in coral; the filter's gain in ink over the paper-2 voice band, the cutoff in teal (the page's "cutoff: half gain" mark), the whistle crossed in coral, the filter box teal (`k-box-good`); the mirrored copy in the next bug as accent-soft with a dashed coral edge. Teal tick and coral cross only beside a word. Labels in Geist Mono at 48 px or more, the quote in Instrument Serif. Motion grammar: waves scroll like a live line, stems grow with a soft landing, wheels turn once and then step one sample at a time, curves reveal left to right, the strip folds once; held scenes drift (scale 1 to 1.03, the hero 1.04). Hard cuts between scenes. Every element sits above y = 900, clear of the caption band.

Hero frame: scene 7, the fold: the tent drawn by a coral marker sweeping the true tone from 0 to 8,000 Hz, the limit landing on "limit", the alias point on "alias", then the strip of true tones folding at 4,000 Hz so 5,000 lands under 3,000, with "1,000 above" and "1,000 below" on their words. The sweep starts at 84.5 s (58%); the fold lands at 95.3 s and 5,000 reaches 3,000 at 100.6 s, 66 to 69% of the film.
