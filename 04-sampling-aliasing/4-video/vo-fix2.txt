The sound going into the phone is the true tone, a smooth wobble. But a phone only works with numbers. Eight thousand times a second, it takes one sample. That's the sampling rate. In between, it looks away. The list of samples is what the phone stores, and nothing else survives.
The fix has to come before sampling, while five thousand is still five thousand. Put a filter in front of the sampler, a small circuit that keeps the voice, up to three thousand four hundred hertz, and removes the tones above it. The tone where it switches from keeping to removing is its cutoff. The whistle never reaches the sampler, and the clinic gets honest silence.
