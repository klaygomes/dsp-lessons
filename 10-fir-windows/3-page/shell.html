The Ghost in the Sweep

A sweep from 4,000 to 8,000 Hz should come out of lesson 5’s filter as silence. Instead a ghost glides down into the range where voices live, swelling and fading. How does a filter that only multiplies and adds let it through, and even make some tones louder?
