Lesson six split a real tone into two half-size arrows: the top one counter-clockwise, its mirror clockwise. A clockwise dot is a negative tone, and sounds like its positive twin.
The Hilbert transformer's coefficients never end, like lesson ten's ideal filter. Keep thirty-one, taper them with a [HH AA1 N] window: it runs fifteen samples late. Delay the plain call to match, or the ghost is back: forty-one percent at a thousand hertz.
