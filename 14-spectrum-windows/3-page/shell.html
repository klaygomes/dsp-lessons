The Keys That Blur

A loud 4 lights up the bin that belongs to 7, and a faint key never scores high enough to count. The bins are 31.25 Hz apart and the keypad tones 73 Hz or more apart. So where does a tone's sound go?

Source: Alan V. Oppenheim and Ronald W. Schafer, Discrete-Time Signal Processing, Prentice Hall, 1989, chapter 10. Next lesson: the Hilbert transform, where a voice shifted up 300 Hz grows a ghost underneath. Lab drawing adapted from the Sample Hold Ladder study in the iwrzwr visual archive (MIT).
