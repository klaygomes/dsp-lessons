# Storyboard: Ghosts on the Wideband Line

**Problem:** your line now joins a 16,000 samples/s wideband line through a bridge, and every voice from your line arrives with a thin, high ghost above 4,000 Hz that goes down when the voice goes up. **Audience:** software engineers with no signal processing background who did lessons 1 to 4. **Goal:** the viewer can say what upsampling by 2 does (a zero between every two samples), why the zeros are the tone at half height plus an image at 8,000 Hz minus the tone (even samples agree, odd samples cancel; on the wheel, a flip then 22.5° back), and where the low-pass filter goes each way: after the zeros going up (gain 2), before dropping samples going down (gain 1). **Emotion:** from "the zeros are harmless silence" to the surprise that silence is two tones, then the calm of a bridge that adds nothing above 4,000 Hz.

Voice: the lesson 1 narrator, reused from its saved speaker (`01-signals-and-systems/4-video/vo/voice.pt`, designed as `female, young adult, high pitch, american accent`), for series continuity. Exact command:

```bash
agent-voice script 05-changing-the-rate/4-video/vo.txt --out 05-changing-the-rate/4-video/vo --prompt 01-signals-and-systems/4-video/vo/voice.pt --speed 1.1 --temperature 0.8
```

`--speed 1.1 --temperature 0.8` is the user's choice by ear. Two non-verbal tags: `[surprise-oh]` at the reveal ("A whole voice comes back upside down", line 7) and `[sigh]` before the next ticket (line 10). No tag reaches the captions or the screen.

Visual code, the same marks as the page's figures (`2-figures/figures.mjs`, `lesson.css`): the hum and every voice tone as a filled ink dot with a solid ink spoke; the 7,000 Hz tone and every image as a hollow coral ring with a dashed coral spoke; shadows as dotted drops to the horizontal line; samples as ink stems with filled dots, the zeros as hollow ink circles on the axis; the true tone as a dashed ink-soft curve; half of 1,000 Hz as ink-soft triangles on dashed stems, half of 7,000 Hz as coral rings on dotted stems; the meter's ghost bar and the image band hatched coral with a dashed coral edge, the voice band paper-2 with an ink edge; the mirror line at 4,000 Hz dashed ink-soft; the ideal step a thin ink-soft line, the neighbor average dotted, the 31-sample rule solid ink, the spec as input-soft boxes (the target figure); the low-pass boxes accent-soft with a coral border (the bridge figure's `l5-box-accent`); the 2,000 Hz squeal as a dotted coral curve (the downsample figure). Teal tick and teal text only for "agrees", "same shadow", "a real tone" and a passed spec. Labels in Geist Mono at 48 px or more, the customer's words in Instrument Serif. Motion grammar: spectra flicker like a live meter, stems grow with a soft landing and drop from one strip to the next, wheels turn on their words, curves reveal left to right, the image band unfolds across the mirror; held scenes drift (scale 1 to 1.03, the hero 1.04). Hard cuts between scenes. Every element sits above y = 900, clear of the caption band.

Hero frame: scene 7, the mirror: a tone dot and its image ring slide in opposite directions on "every", the "3,000" distances land on "mirrored", then the voice band appears on "whole" and its image unfolds upside down across 4,000 Hz on "upside", with "4,600 to 7,700 Hz" on its word.
